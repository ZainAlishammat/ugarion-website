What we do

Ugarion builds real-time systems for industry, audio and video – hardware and software from a single source, with EtherCAT® at the core. Founded in Munich in October 2026: we are at the beginning, and we build on measurements, not promises.

  • EtherCAT® MainDevice

    Our own master stack in native Rust, from Linux PCs to embedded processors. 10 µs cycle time on Linux. Clock sync within 3 ns (p99.9). Measured on real hardware.

  • EtherCAT® SubDevice

    Our own SubDevice firmware for 100 Mbit/s and 1 Gbit/s, with 165 ns cut-through at 1 Gbit/s – the foundation for our own devices.

  • Acoustic imaging & audio zoom In development

    We make sound visible: our AI-powered acoustic imaging pinpoints noise sources in machines and plants in real time, and audio zoom isolates a single source from the noise. Live as an EtherCAT® SubDevice or via Audio over IP (AES67); multiple units can be cascaded.

  • AI at the edge In development

    Our own PLC and HMI bring AI right next to the real-time control loop, on chips with built-in AI accelerators: detecting anomalies, finding faults faster, open to AI assistants via our API.

  • Engineering services

    We bring real-time into your product: embedded Linux, RTOS, drivers, real-time networking and EtherCAT® integration.
